Privacy policy

Updated 22 October 2026. This is the shared privacy policy for Slightly Puzzled on iOS (including TestFlight) and Android (including Google Play testing). The same public policy URL is used for both platforms and future app updates; the practices described below reflect the current release.

Your puzzle records

Your puzzle results, Brain Score, streak, history, recent-puzzle records, language, reminder preferences and sound settings are stored locally on your device. The app has no player account, cloud-save service, analytics service or AI service. We do not receive those puzzle records through the app.

Records remain until removed through your operating system's app-data controls. Uninstall and restoration behaviour, including device backups, depend on the operating system and your backup settings. There is no cross-device synchronisation service operated by us.

Children and families

Slightly Puzzled is intended for all ages, including children under 13. In the current release, children and adults can play without creating an account or submitting personal information to us. Puzzle progress and settings stay on the device. We do not use in-app behavioral advertising, tracking or analytics for children or adults in this release.

A parent or guardian can manage local app data and notification permissions through the device settings. If a child needs support, a parent or guardian should contact us rather than asking the child to send personal information. If you believe we have received a child's personal information, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where appropriate. Optional platform feedback and third-party sharing are described below.

Advertising and purchases

The current release does not enable advertising requests or in-app purchases. Advertising and purchase libraries are included in the app, but their production services are not configured or initialised for this release. Before enabling those features, we will update this policy and the store disclosures to explain their processing and available choices.

Reminders and sharing

Optional reminders are scheduled locally with your permission. You can turn them off in the app or your device settings. Sharing a result is initiated by you through the system share sheet; the app you choose receives the shared image under its own policies.

TestFlight and platform diagnostics

Apple or Google may process downloads, device diagnostics and store interactions under their own policies and your platform settings. If you submit TestFlight feedback, Apple makes the feedback and associated device or contact information available to us. We use it to investigate issues and improve the game. Sending feedback is optional.

If you contact us

If you email us, we process your address, message and any attachments to answer you and resolve the issue. Please avoid including sensitive information. We retain correspondence only as long as reasonably needed for support or applicable legal requirements. Our basis for ordinary support correspondence is our legitimate interest in responding to requests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

This website

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. We add no analytics scripts or advertising cookies. GitHub may process technical request information to operate and secure the hosting service. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Your rights

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object to processing of personal data we hold about you. Contact us using the address above. You may also complain to your local data protection supervisory authority. Local puzzle records are controlled on your device; we cannot retrieve or delete them remotely.

Changes

We will update this page when the app's data practices change and show the revised date. This URL remains the policy link for both iOS and Android. If a future version changes how data is handled, including by enabling advertising, purchases or cloud features, we will update this page and the relevant store disclosures before introducing that processing. Older installed versions may continue to have the local-only behavior described here.
