Slightly Puzzled support

Small puzzles. Satisfying little aha moments.

Need help or found a puzzle that does not seem right? Email effskydusty@gmail.com.

Reporting a problem

Tell us your app version, device model, puzzle type and what happened. A screenshot and the steps that led to the issue are useful. Do not include passwords or payment details. TestFlight players can also use Send Beta Feedback in TestFlight.

Sound and reminders

Open Settings to turn sound on or off and adjust effects volume in the latest preliminary build. Reminders are optional; you can disable them in the game or in your device's notification settings.

Your progress

Progress and preferences are saved on this device. The current app has no sign-in or cloud sync. Deleting app data may remove your progress; backup and uninstall behaviour depend on your device settings.

About this release

This is an early release. Daily Mix and unlimited practice are available; advertising and purchases are not enabled in the current preliminary build. Brain Score is a game score, not a medical assessment.
